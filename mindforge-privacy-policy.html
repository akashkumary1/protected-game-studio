MindForge Puzzle — Privacy Policy

App: MindForge Puzzle (Android package com.mindforge.app) · Developer: Protected Game Studio · Effective: 18 November 2026 · Applies to version 2.8.0 and later

Summary: MindForge Puzzle is an exam-practice and puzzle app (CTET, UPTET, Super TET, SSC, police, banking and other mock tests, reasoning puzzles and an on-device study coach). Practice, tests, results and study data stay on your phone. The app sends nothing over the internet when it starts. Data leaves your phone only if you choose to use Online 2-Player (friend invitations, chat, image sharing and voice) or tap Check for Updates for the weekly knowledge pack. There are no ads, no analytics and no account or login.

1. What the app does

  • Exam test series: timed mock tests, subject tests and answer review for teaching and competitive exams, in English and Hindi. Questions are practice items on frequently asked topics; they are not official papers.
  • Puzzles: aptitude and reasoning puzzles in several game modes.
  • Smart Coach: finds weak topics, keeps a Mistake Book and plans spaced revision. All of this is calculated and stored on your phone.
  • Optional online multiplayer: play a 2-player match with a friend using a Friend Code or room code, with optional text chat, image sharing and voice chat.
  • Optional weekly knowledge pack: current-affairs practice questions that you can download.

2. Data stored only on your phone

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • Puzzle progress, XP, levels and statistics
  • Test attempts, scores and answer reviews
  • Smart Coach data: topic accuracy, the Mistake Book and the revision schedule
  • Settings: language, theme and sound
  • Your Friend Code and a random installation secret (used only if you go online)

You can delete it with Settings → Reset All Progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

3. Data processed only when you use Online 2-Player

Nothing in this section happens until you open Online 2-Player.

3.1 Friend invitations (our invitation service)

To let friends invite you while the app is closed, the app registers with our invitation service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers. For each phone it stores:

  • your Friend Code (8 random characters, not your name or phone number);
  • the Firebase Cloud Messaging notification token for this installation;
  • a SHA-256 hash of the installation secret (the secret itself is not stored);
  • time of the last registration and last invitation (used for rate limiting);
  • a pending invitation (sender's Friend Code and room code), which expires after 5 minutes.

Retention: the registration is kept while you use online invitations and is not deleted automatically. Email us your Friend Code (shown on the Online 2-Player screen) and we will delete it. Request logging on the service is switched off.

3.2 Notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Invitation notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Firebase starts only after you open Online 2-Player. Google processes the notification token and delivery data under the Firebase privacy terms. The app asks for the Android notification permission at that point; you can refuse it and still play by sharing a room code.

3.3 Connecting a match (PeerJS, STUN and TURN)

Matches are peer-to-peer (WebRTC). To find each other, the two phones use the public PeerJS signalling service, Google and Cloudflare STUN servers, and, on networks that block direct connections, the PeerJS TURN relay. These services see your IP address and a random connection ID for the duration of the connection. Relayed traffic is encrypted.

3.4 Chat, images and voice

  • Text messages, images you choose to share and voice audio travel directly between the two phones over an encrypted WebRTC connection. They are not stored on our servers, and messages disappear when the match ends.
  • Microphone: used only for a voice call that you start or accept during a match. The app asks for the microphone permission at that moment; you can refuse and keep playing without voice.
  • Images: chosen by you with Android's system file or photo picker; the app has no storage permission and does not read your gallery.
  • Your opponent can screenshot or record what you send. Play only with people you know, and never share passwords, personal documents or contact details.

4. Weekly knowledge pack

When you tap Check for Updates, the app downloads a public question pack from our Cloudflare service. No personal data is sent; as with any internet request, the server sees your IP address. At launch the app uses only the pack bundled with it or saved earlier.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No account, login, name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • No sale or sharing of data for marketing.
  • Android System WebView's Safe Browsing checks, usage metrics and form autofill are switched off for the app.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: optional online features and the update check only.
  • Notifications: friend invitations; requested when you open Online 2-Player.
  • Microphone and audio settings: voice chat in a match; requested when you start or accept a call.

7. Children

The offline practice and puzzle features are suitable for all ages and collect nothing. Online 2-Player includes unmoderated chat, image sharing and voice with the person you are matched with through a code; there is no public matchmaking. Children should use online features only with a parent's or guardian's permission and supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used online invitations, email us the Friend Code and we will delete the registration.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS or encrypted WebRTC. The invitation service stores only a hash of the installation secret and checks it before accepting invitations. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share sensitive information in chat.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app fully offline by never opening Online 2-Player.
  • Turn off notifications or microphone access at any time in Android settings.
  • Request deletion of your invitation-service registration by email (include your Friend Code). We respond within 30 days.

10. Exam content disclaimer

Practice questions are written for preparation on topics that recur in previous papers. They are not official question papers or predictions, and MindForge Puzzle is not affiliated with any examination authority.

11. Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing that version.
